CivicOne Redressal Engine Walkthrough

Public Sector SLA Gating, Jurisdiction Scoping & Citizen Confirmation

1

Deterministic Intake & PII Sanitization

Your raw complaint is scrubbed with PiiMasker to redact Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers, and emails before field workers inspect the case.

2

GeoRouter & Priority Scoring Engine

GeoRouter resolves the geographic ward and nearest civic asset. PriorityScorer computes a deterministic priority score (e.g. Life-safety wire hazard: 6h SLA).

3

Resolution Evidence & Citizen Confirmation Gate

Officers cannot close cases arbitrarily. Resolution proof photos must be attached, and the citizen receives a confirmation prompt. If disputed, the case automatically reopens.
